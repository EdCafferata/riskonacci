What does the app do?

  • Multiple decks: Fibonacci, Standard, T-shirt sizes or a dedicated Risk deck (None→Critical)
  • Likelihood × Impact: vote in two rounds, result in a 5×5 risk matrix (green→red)
  • Play together, anywhere: share a 5-character room ID, everyone votes live
  • Runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac — in 10 languages
  • No account, no sign-in, no ads
  • Free, with an optional tip jar
