FAQ

  • Do I need an account? No. Each device gets a random ID; no sign-in needed.
  • How do we play together? The host shares a 5-character room ID; participants join and see the same live vote.
  • What is the Risk deck? A dedicated card type (None, Low, Medium, High, Critical) with a Likelihood×Impact matrix.
  • What does it cost? Nothing. Riskonacci is free; a tip jar is optional.
